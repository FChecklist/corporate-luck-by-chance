How Corporate Luck by Chance plays

One number. One dice. Ten rounds.

A dice round

1

The target

A number from 1 to 6 appears on every screen.

2

You press

You have about 10 seconds to press the dice.

3

The reveal

Every dice shows at the same moment.

4

The points

If your dice shows the target, you score that many points. If not, you score zero.

Rounds

10 dice rounds in a round

About two minutes.

10 rounds in a game

About 26 minutes in all. The organizer can choose 1 to 20 rounds.

Points add up

Your total keeps growing across the rounds.

Example: the target is 4. You roll a 4: you score 4. You roll a 2: you score 0.

Prizes

Each round

First to fifth of every round, and a top-ten list.

Overall

First to fifth of the total, and a top-ten list.

Your choice

The organizer picks round prizes, overall prizes, or both.

No ties

With thousands of players, ties are certain, so every game has a clear order. If players are level on points, the highest single hit decides, then the most hits, then who reached the score first. The last measure is a random draw anyone can re-check.

Optional golden shoot-out: if players are still level for a top-five place, extra golden dice rounds are played among only those players until they separate.

How random is random?

The target and each dice come from a public random source. The target does not exist until the previous dice round ends. Your dice does not exist until your press window closes. Nobody can know a result in advance, not even us. Anyone can re-compute every result afterwards.

Two ways to play

Fast mode

The dice rolls itself when the window closes. It works for thousands and every phone shows the same standings.

Strict mode

You must press inside the window or you score zero for that dice round. This is for smaller games.

Corporate Luck by Chance

Press the dice. Hit the number. Ten rounds of pure luck.
