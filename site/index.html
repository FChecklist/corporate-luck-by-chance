Corporate Luck by Chance

A number appears. Everyone presses the dice. Hit the number and win its points. Ten rounds of pure luck, with thousands of players at once.

How it playsTell me when it opens

How a game works

1

A target appears

A big number from 1 to 6 shows on every screen.

2

Press the dice

Everyone presses within a few seconds.

3

Hit it, score it

Roll the target number and score that many points. Anything else scores zero.

4

Ten rounds

Each round has ten dice rounds. Points keep adding up to the final winner.

Why companies like it

Simple as it gets

One number, one dice. Anyone can play in seconds.

Truly random

The target and every dice come from a public random source nobody can predict, not even us.

Thousands at once

The same dice round for every player at the same moment.

Prizes every round

Round prizes and an overall prize, with a live top-ten list.

Clear winners

A strict 1st to 5th and a top-ten list. No ties.

Nicknames

Players use nicknames. Real names stay private.

Clear winners, always

1st to 5th, always

Every game ends with a clear first, second, third, fourth and fifth place.

A top-ten list

Places six to ten are listed too.

No ties

If players are level, the next measure decides. The last measure is a random draw anyone can re-check.

Told in one line

The results say how any tie was decided.

With thousands of players, ties are certain. So every game has a tie chain and an optional golden shoot-out for the top five places.

Questions

Is there any skill?

No. It is pure luck, and we say so.

How long does a game last?

A dice round takes about 13 seconds. Ten rounds of ten dice rounds is about 26 minutes.

Do players pay?

No. The organizer pays.

Is it fair?

Yes. Every player has exactly a 1 in 6 chance every time. The dice cannot be known before the window closes.

How many can play?

Thousands in the fast mode. A strict mode where you must press in time suits smaller games.
