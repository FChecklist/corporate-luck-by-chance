Rules, privacy and disclaimer

Short and plain. Your organizer runs your game. Version 2026-10-15.

Rules

  • A game has 1 to 20 rounds (ten by default) and every round has 10 dice rounds. In each dice round a target number from 1 to 6 appears and every player presses the dice. A dice that shows the target scores that many points; any other number scores nothing.
  • The ranking has no ties. The most points come first, then the highest single hit, then the most hits, then who reached the score first. If players are still equal, fate decides.
  • Fate is a number made from the game, the seat and a public random number. The lower number wins, and anyone can re-check it.
  • Every target and every dice is made from a public random number that does not exist until its moment. The fingerprint of the game's sealed secret is public before anyone joins. The secret is shown after the game, so anyone can re-check every number.
  • Players have a press window of 6, 10 or 15 seconds, as the organizer chooses. The dice is rolled for everybody when the window closes, so a player who is away still scores.
  • A player who loses the connection can open the link again; the screen catches up by itself.
  • Every round has its own ranking and the overall result uses the running total. Prizes are gifts the organizer provides; a prize is never money.
  • Places 1 to 5 are named and a top 10 is listed. The results say in one line how each tie was decided.
  • The software decides every result. Its result is final.
  • The organizer and we have no way to change a draw or a result in the software.
  • Players never pay and never stake money. There are no cash prizes and no betting. Any gift is supplied by the organizer, outside the product.
  • Players are 18 or older, and the game is for company events.

Privacy & Data Policy

  • We delete all participant data from our systems within 24 hours after the game ends.
  • A game that never starts is treated the same way: its guests’ names and numbers are deleted 23 hours after the time it was meant to end.
  • All data is encrypted, in transit and at rest.
  • Where the data is: the website runs on Cloudflare’s global network. The database is kept in Cloudflare’s Asia-Pacific region.
  • Participants never see each other’s data.
  • A player can leave at any time, ask what we hold about them, or ask us to delete it, from the game page. We answer within 7 days.
  • A player can also name another person who may use these rights for them (DPDP Act, Section 14), from the game page or by writing to hello@corporatetambola.com.
  • We provide the software. The organizer runs the game. Partners who refer organizers follow the partner terms.
  • The full notice is on its own page, the Privacy notice. It is available in Hindi on request. The providers we use are on the sub-processors page.
  • No advertising cookies or trackers. We never sell data.
  • Your phone keeps only the items listed under “What your device keeps” below. We use no other cookie.
  • Exception: the final places are kept for 365 days as seat numbers, without names, and are then deleted automatically.
  • We keep billing data for audit and tax reasons.
  • Fair play. We keep a protected, encrypted record of game activity to keep every game fair. Only we can see it. Removed automatically 365 days after your game ends. The record of an anonymous visitor (a request that was refused or failed) keeps only the first part of the network address and is removed after 90 days.
  • Public pages: a first-party visitor id and shortened network address, never names or mobile numbers, kept 365 days. With Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we only count visits.
  • Designed to follow India’s DPDP Act 2023, the EU GDPR and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA.
  • Your phone contacts YouTube (privacy mode) only if your organizer adds a video.
  • Guests are never e-mailed. Only the organizer and Hosts have an e-mail address on file.
  • Our hosting provider encrypts the database at rest. Names and numbers are also sealed with AES-256 inside it. From the moment a game starts, nicknames are also held as plain text so that every phone can show the top-10 list; they are deleted with the rest, 23 hours after the game ends. We store no payment card details.
  • You may also complain to a data protection authority, such as the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Questions or requests: our Grievance Officer, Rajat Agarwal, hello@corporatetambola.com. We reply within 30 days.

What your device keeps

  • On a player’s phone: a random device id, so one place stays on one phone.
  • On a player’s phone: the player id for each game and the last game opened, so the game comes back after a reload.
  • In an organizer’s browser, only when the console shows a trial of the phone page: a trial player’s id, device id and progress.
  • In an organizer’s browser tab, until the tab is closed: the sign-in, the e-mail and mobile number typed, and the page to open next.
  • In an organizer’s browser: a sign-in token and a random device id for the console.
  • In an organizer’s browser: the name, e-mail, mobile number and invoice details typed on the payment page, so the form is filled in next time.
  • In an organizer’s browser: which setup steps are done and which messages were sent, for each game.
  • On the Refer & earn page: the partner’s key, so the page opens again.
  • After a Refer & earn link: the partner’s code, so a purchase is credited to the partner.
  • The colour choice.
  • Whether the phone was already asked about notifications.
  • On the public pages only: a random visitor id and one first-party cookie, cbv, kept one year, to count visits. Never on game pages, never with names or numbers. With Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track no id is made.
  • On the public pages only, until the tab is closed: a visit number and what was already counted.
  • On any page: if something goes wrong while the phone is offline, a short error report waits here until the phone is online, is then sent and removed. It holds no names, numbers or typed text.
  • On any page, in this tab only: when we send you to fix something, a note of where you were working, so one tap brings you back. It is removed when you come back or close the tab.
  • The only cookie: cbv, a random visitor id, first party, kept one year, on the public pages only.

No refunds, credit instead

  • We do not give refunds. If you cancel a paid game before it starts, your payment stays as credit for your next game for 365 days. Unused credit lapses at the end of day 365.
  • After you pay, you can move the game to any date within 365 days of the payment. Your payment stays with that game.
  • If you use the credit for another game first, or the 365 days pass, the game cannot be moved. The credit stays for a new game until it lapses.
  • The credit stays in the organizer’s account, and we e-mail the organizer before it lapses.
  • Once the start time has passed, a paid game cannot be cancelled. A game never paid for can be cancelled at any time, and nothing is charged.
  • A game that was not paid for closes when its guest data is erased, 23 hours after it was meant to end. See the Terms.

Accessibility

  • We want everyone to be able to use Corporate Luck by Chance, on a phone, a tablet or a laptop.
  • You can use the pages with a keyboard alone. The Tab key reaches every button, link and field, and the place you are at is outlined.
  • Text and buttons are checked for enough contrast, and every field has a label.
  • Headings, labels and live announcements help a screen reader follow the game.
  • We check this automatically before each release. If something stops you, write to hello@corporatetambola.com and we will fix it or find another way for you.
