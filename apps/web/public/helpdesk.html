Help Desk

Set up the game. They publish. You earn.

Know an Organizer who needs help? Prepare their Corporate Luck by Chance game for them. When it is ready, they check it and publish with one tap.

  • You work in their account, with the same console they use.
  • You can never publish or pay. Only the Organizer does.
  • After they publish, you become a Host of that game.
  • When the game is paid, you earn Refer & earn commission.
  • The Organizer can stop it at any time.

How it works

1

Join Refer & earn

Use your own e-mail. Then turn on Help Desk on this page.

2

Add the Organizer

Type their e-mail. We send them a short note. They read the promise and tap Allow.

3

Prepare the game

Open the console, or give your AI link to your AI assistant. Set the time, the prizes and the guests.

4

Ask them to publish

Tap one button. They get an e-mail with a summary. They confirm and tap Publish.

Questions people ask

Who can be a Help Desk person?

Anyone who has joined Refer & earn. Help Desk is a switch on the same account.

What can I do in the Organizer’s account?

Create a game and change its settings and prizes. That is all. You cannot publish, pay, see sign-in keys, change the e-mail or close the account.

What do I see about the guests?

Only what you type yourself. After the Organizer publishes, you are a Host of that game and see what a Host sees.

How do I earn?

Every paid game you prepared earns you Refer & earn commission, at the same rate and with the same rules as any other sale. Buying through your own number does not earn.

What if the Organizer changes their mind?

They stop it with one tap. Your access ends at once. Games they already published stay as they are.
