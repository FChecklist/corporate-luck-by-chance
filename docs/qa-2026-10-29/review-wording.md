Base folder for every file:line below (read-only checkout, nothing edited, nothing committed):

How to read the severity column.
- HIGH = breaks an owner rule in text a PLAYER can see (player screens, offline page, install dialog, or a legal page the join screen links to: privacy.html and terms.html are linked from luck.js:34 and big.js:21) and the sentence is wrong or misleading.
- MEDIUM = an organizer, a Host, a visitor or an e-mail reader sees it.
- LOW = style, jargon, dead text, or a decision for the owner rather than a defect.
- "Not deployed": `site/` is a "coming soon" site (site/README.md, pages/build.mjs ships only apps/web/public). Its text is reviewed because it was in scope.
- Last column: YES = wording only, no behaviour change, no test pins it (or the pinning test is named). NO = a test pins it, or it needs an owner/legal decision, or it is a behaviour change.
- Two tests tie wording together: `apps/web/test/legal-sync.test.ts` (terms, legal-position, refund-policy, privacy in apps/web/public must equal the same page in site/, word for word), and `tools/make-trust.ts` plus its test (trust.html and llms.txt are generated from apps/edge/src/ai-facts.ts and a test fails on drift).

Key fact behind many rows (verified in code): the product is "Fate mode only". `packages/luck/src/engine.ts:105-110` computes the dice of EVERY seat from the second public beacon value, whether or not the player pressed. The Press button never reaches the server (no press route in apps/edge/src/luck.ts). So pressing is only a picture, nobody "misses" a round, there is no pause (docs/RULES.md R51, worker.ts:1092), and the server holds the sealed secret and can compute every number (docs/RULES.md "Known limits", trust.html:20, llms.txt).

Totals: 63 findings: 12 HIGH, 34 MEDIUM, 17 LOW. (By rule: the word "private" in 17 findings (about 27 places), W1 and W13 to W28; payee rules are clean except W9, W12, W58 to W61; the many wording-versus-code gaps are W2 to W11 and W29 to W57.)

Read first (what a player or buyer could be misled by):
1. W2, W10, W29, W30: "nobody can know a result in advance, not even us" is false; the server holds the sealed secret (the Trust page already admits it).
2. W3: players are told to press the dice, but the dice rolls for everybody anyway; nothing is lost by not pressing or pressing late.
3. W9: the Terms say cash prizes are allowed at the organizer's risk; the product and every other page say gifts only, never money.
4. W4, W5, W6, W7, W8: five small false or foreign sentences on player-visible screens (deletion of "your place", "winning claims", the offline page, the install text, ticket/mobile-number steps).
5. W33: about 20 places say the game starts by itself (on time, 2 minutes after payment); only a press of Start by the Organizer or a Host starts it.
