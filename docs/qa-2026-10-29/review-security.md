No critical finding: I found no way to read the secret/seed before a game is over, no cross-tenant read of personal data, no SQL injection, no XSS, no committed secret, and no way to forge a Razorpay payment. The serious problems are (1) several ways to get a game, a bigger pack or credit **without paying**, (2) the **result** of a game can be decided by one person, (3) **old Bingo-era routes** that trust a public card id, (4) the **daily write budget** and the **free-plan request quota** are easy to exhaust, and (5) **organizer accounts can be pre-claimed**.

## C. Checked and found sound (so you do not need to re-check)

**Fairness and secrecy of the draw**
* A beacon value is never served before it is due: `SEC/e22_sound.ts` asks every second from 5 s before the start to the end (207 looks): 0 early values, 0 early reveals. The dice value (odd index) appears only when the press window closes (`luck.ts:182-188`, `packages/luck/src/timeline.ts:17`).
* The secret and the seed appear only in `reveal` after `state='done'`; `done` is reached only after the game end (votes before it are refused 409, `luck.ts:202`) or the 10-minute no-agreement timeout. `SEC/e21_fuzz.ts` (about 240 hostile requests: odd types, huge numbers, injection strings, malformed bodies, 200 KB bodies) found 0 server errors and 0 leaks of secret/seed in `view`, `seats`, `rank`, `reveal`, `beacon`.
* Commit-then-reveal is implemented as documented (`commitOf`, `seedOf`, `beaconValue`).

**Authorization** (`SEC/e10_authz.ts`: 38 routes x 8 roles, every cell on a fresh database)
* Only Organizer, Host (console) and platform may start, end or tick; AI key, Co-host, Host-via-AI-link, another organizer and strangers get 403. Platform-only routes (`/api/owner/*`, `/api/lifecycle/tick`, `/api/purge`, `/api/org`, `/api/notices`) answer 404/403 to everyone else. The AI key cannot publish, pay, erase, cancel, clone, make pay links, add Hosts, read keys or the lasting AI link. Co-host is limited to its own people list, pay link, payment and header. Another organizer's key gets 403 on every game route.
* Player routes (`luck/me`, `luck/vote`, `confirm`, `myprizes`, `leave`, `datareq`) check pid plus device; wrong device answers 403 and `me` returns only the caller's seat. Pids and devices are scoped by game id.
* Sign-in: codes are random 6 digits, hashed, 10 minutes, 5 attempts; link tokens 192 bits, hashed, single use; organizer keys 192 bits, stored hashed; all random values come from `crypto.getRandomValues` (`Math.random` is used only for sampling).

**Injection and output**
* SQL: every `prepare()` is parameterised; the template-literal ones (`people.ts`, `admin.ts`, `credit.ts`, `luck.ts:220`, `visits.ts`, `ledgerc.ts`, `blackbox.ts`, `lifecycle.ts:96`) interpolate only constants or `?` lists.
* XSS: I reviewed every `innerHTML` sink in the shipped scripts (about 830 interpolations, automated listing in `SEC/interp.py`, then by hand). Nicknames, result lines, prize texts, company name, error messages, invoice and mail HTML are escaped; announcements use `textContent`; the logo is accepted only as `data:image/(png|jpeg|webp);base64` and the server checks its real bytes, size and dimensions; brand colour is one of eight fixed values. PDF text is escaped; CSV export guards formula prefixes; `GET /j/<token>` has its own strict CSP.
* Headers actually served come from `pages/build.mjs:19-37`: CSP with `script-src 'self'` (plus Cloudflare Insights, Razorpay on payment pages, Google only on sign-in), `frame-ancestors 'none'`, `base-uri 'none'`, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `nosniff`, `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, both `/x` and `/x.html` covered. Navigation targets after sign-in are allow-listed (no open redirect). The payment frame checks both `origin` and `source` of messages (`apps/web/public/common.js:141`).

**Secrets and personal data**
* No secret, key, token or private data is committed (pattern scan of all 426 tracked files and the 50 commits of history: Razorpay, Resend, GitHub, AWS, Slack, JWT, private-key and long-hex patterns). Only public drand constants, the owner's published business contact details, and the D1 database id (`pages/wrangler.toml:11`, an identifier, not a credential). `.gitignore` covers `config/live-secrets.txt`, `config/platform-key.txt`, `.env*`.
* Logging: `console.*` writes nothing personal; the error box scrubs e-mails, numbers and long tokens; the internal audit log is sealed, participant IPs are under a per-game key that is destroyed 23 hours after the game, anonymous visitors keep only a truncated IP for 90 days; visit tracking keeps a shortened IP plus a keyed HMAC and honours Global Privacy Control.
* At rest: names and mobile numbers are AES-256-GCM sealed (HKDF key, random nonce, AAD): `participant.pii`, `person.pii`, `waiting.pii`, `prize.pii`, host/co-host `pii`, `orgmail/`, `aikey/`, report chunks. Plain: `luck_p.name` (nicknames, deleted at 23 h), `luck_v` (deleted at 23 h), `luck_t.secret/seed/result`, organizer e-mail and 10-digit mobile in `kv contact/<game>` (deleted at 365 days), `payment.email` and invoice data (kept for tax), `outbox` addresses and subjects (3 days), `ai_audit` ip/ua (S22). Phone numbers are hashed with a secret salt (`PHONE_SALT`) for matching.
* The 23-hour erasure works as coded: `SEC/e13_retention.ts` shows participants, seats and the sealed list gone after the tick (the only leftover is the planted prize row of S6).

**Payments**
* Signature checks use HMAC-SHA256 with `timingSafeEqual` for both checkout and webhook; `verify` also asks Razorpay for the payment and compares order id and amount; the webhook rejects amount mismatches and dedupes by event id; `fulfil` is idempotent (`ent_<payment>` and invoice rows are unique). The amount is always computed on the server from the pack table; USD is refused unless enabled. Pay-link tokens (192-bit, hashed), invoice tokens (128-bit) and credit-note tokens are unguessable. The seller on every order, page and invoice is "IT Services by Rajat Agarwal" (`packages/billing/src/index.ts:37`, `apps/web/public/common.js:154`); only `COMPANY_JSON` (a deploy variable) can change the legal name.
* Credit application is guarded in SQL (conditional insert plus conditional update with rollback), so double-spending one credit is not possible; the problems are the business-logic gaps S2, S3 and S4.

**Other**
* No server-side request forgery (the only outbound URLs are environment variables and fixed vendor hosts). No third-party runtime libraries. The service worker never touches `/api`. Cron/tick: `/api/lifecycle/tick` needs the platform key or a `CRON_KEY` of 16+ characters. Join: bodies capped at 4 KB, votes at 48 KB, rosters 100 people / 32 KB; a seat insert is one atomic statement with the cap check inside.

## D. Not covered / limits

* I cannot see the live Cloudflare dashboard (secrets, `STRICT_KEYS`, rate-limit rules, plan, log settings, the registered Razorpay account and the central payment service). Everything about those is marked SUSPECTED or INFO.
* Razorpay and Resend were never called; real D1 behaviour (latency, row-write counting) is estimated, not measured.
* The Help Desk and Refer-and-earn features were reviewed by reading, not by exhaustive testing.
* `race_model.py` is a model of the vote race (honest phones spread evenly over 1.05 s after the end); it is not a measurement.
