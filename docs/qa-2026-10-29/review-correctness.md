## Checked and found sound (no defect found)

- Randomness: 60000 draws: chi-square 6.8 for targets and 9.3 for dice (5 degrees of freedom, 5% limit 11.07; one small 6000-draw run gave 12.5 for dice and a repeat was clean, so that was chance; the code is plain rejection sampling with unique labels). Hit rate by target 0.159 to 0.174 (expected 0.167; each is within 2 standard errors). Every face appears as target and as dice. Over 300 games each of 12 seats won overall 21 to 34 times (no seat favoured). `R/uniform-big.ts`, `R/engine-experiments.ts`.
- Ranking: 400 random tie-heavy boards: result is a permutation, obeys points > best > hits > earliest, does not depend on input order, top-10 equals the prefix of the full order, and no tie line appears without a tie. Wording of the line matches R22. `R/engine-experiments.ts` (X3, X4).
- Bundle: `apps/web/public/luck-engine.js` (what phones run) equals `packages/luck/src` on 600 draws, a full 3-round game with both rankings, 5000 phase probes and 4 settings cases (0 differences). `R/bundle-vs-source.ts`.
- Timeline: 9132 probes at every boundary +-1 ms and mid-interval for rounds 1, 2, 3, 10, 20 and windows 6, 10, 15: `phaseAt`, `diceOpenMs`, `windowCloseMs`, `revealEndMs`, `gameEndMs`, `beaconDueMs` agree with an independent oracle; intervals are contiguous; target A_g is never due before the previous reveal ends; B_g only at window close; default game 24 min 40 s; no break after the last round. `R/timeline-oracle.ts`.
- Phones and clocks: three phones with clocks +3 min, -3 min and exact show the same phase as the server over 60 looks each; two cold phones produce identical final results. `R/phone-experiments.test.ts`.
- End after round: idempotent; ending in a break ends at once; ending in the last round is a no-op; the beacon feed stops at the shortened total; the vote must have the shortened number of rounds; `end_ms` moves the no-agreement timer and the deletion clock (`luck-end.test.ts`, `R/lifecycle-experiments.test.ts`).
- Vote rules: shape checks reject unknown seats, repeated seats, wrong number of rounds, impossible scores; votes only after the game end; a wrong phone or non-player is refused.
- Lifecycle: agreed game sets `life` to the agreement time; phones that never agree close at end + 10 min and the clock starts 1 minute later; a game never started still gets its clock (scheduled + 6 h); 23 hours later `participant`, `luck_p`, `luck_v` are gone while `luck_t.result` keeps seat numbers.
- Settings: ranges for rounds, window and mode are enforced; extra keys (fee, price, `diceRounds`, `revealSecs`, `breakSecs`) are ignored, so a client cannot change the timeline; prize text is escaped when drawn; nicknames are HTML-escaped.
- Demo (`demo.js`) uses the engine, not its own draw.

## Not covered
